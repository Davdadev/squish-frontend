About Us

We make playful, high-quality 3D printed fidgets in Australia. Every item is designed and printed with care, checked for smooth movement, and packed with love — for kids, adults, and everyone in between.

Our fidgets are popular with families managing ADHD, autism, anxiety, and sensory processing needs, as well as anyone who just loves something fun to click, spin, or squish.

Our Prices

We keep our prices fair and reflective of the quality of our products. Each fidget is priced below our competitors so you get the same craftsmanship at a better price. Every fidget is made to order using premium PLA filament.
